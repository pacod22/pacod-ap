# VAT Registration

## Mandatory registration

A person carrying out independent economic activity in Bulgaria is required
to register for VAT once their taxable turnover for the past 12 consecutive
months reaches the threshold set by Article 96 of the VAT Act. The
threshold is statutorily defined and updated by amendment.

## Filing window

A registration application must be filed within seven days after the end of
the tax period in which the threshold was reached. Registration is performed
by the National Revenue Agency (NRA).

## Voluntary registration

A person whose turnover is below the threshold may register voluntarily.
This is common for freelancers — software developers, translators,
consultants — working with foreign clients, since voluntary registration
lets them deduct VAT charged on business inputs.

## Consequences of registration

A registered person:
- charges VAT on their supplies;
- is entitled to deduct input VAT on business purchases;
- files a monthly VAT return by the 14th day of the following month;
- maintains a sales ledger and a purchases ledger.

## Deregistration

Voluntary deregistration is possible if turnover for the past 12 months
falls below the threshold. Deregistration is mandatory upon termination
of the activity.
