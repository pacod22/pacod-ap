# Rental Income for Individuals

## Tax base

For individual landlords, the taxable rental income is calculated by
deducting statutory recognised expenses from gross rent. The expense
deduction percentage is set by Article 31, paragraph 1 of the PIT Act.
The remaining amount is subject to the flat personal income tax.

## Advance tax

Where the tenant is a business or a self-employed person, the tenant must
withhold advance tax on the rent paid and remit it to the NRA, issuing a
withholding certificate to the landlord.

Where the tenant is an individual (a residential tenant), the landlord
remits advance tax themselves on a quarterly basis, by the end of the
month following the quarter. No advance tax is due for the fourth quarter —
final reconciliation happens via the annual return.

## Filing

Rental income is declared in the annual return (Article 50 of the PIT
Act) on Schedule 4. Rental contracts and any withholding certificates
are attached.

## Short-term-let exemption

Income from letting one's own home for up to 14 consecutive days per year
(very small-scale tourist letting under a specified limit) may be exempt
under specific conditions. A specific consultation with an accountant is
recommended for the particular case.
