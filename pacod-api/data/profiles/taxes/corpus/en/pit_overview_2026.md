# Personal Income Tax in Bulgaria (2026)

## Who is liable

Bulgarian tax residents — individuals living in Bulgaria for more than 183
days in a calendar year — are taxed on their worldwide income. Non-residents
are taxed only on Bulgarian-source income.

## Headline rate

Bulgaria operates a flat personal income tax. The same rate applies to all
levels of income, after the deduction of mandatory social security
contributions and statutory allowances.

## Categories of taxable income

Taxable income includes:
- employment income;
- income from sole-trader activity;
- income from civil-law contracts (freelance services);
- rental income from real property;
- income from the sale of property;
- bank interest income (under specific conditions);
- dividend income.

## Non-taxable items

Among items that are not subject to personal income tax: proceeds from the
sale of a sole residence held for more than three years; education
scholarships; benefits paid by the state social security system; winnings
from state-organised lotteries.

## Filing

The annual personal income tax return (Article 50 of the PIT Act) is filed
between 10 January and 30 April of the following year. Self-employed
individuals filing electronically by 31 March and paying within the same
deadline are entitled to an early-filing discount on the tax due.

## Interaction with social security

Mandatory social security contributions paid by the individual are deducted
from gross income before the flat tax is applied. This is why the tax base
is lower than gross income.
