# Self-Employment Social Security

## Who counts as self-employed

Under the Bulgarian Social Security Code, self-employed persons include:
- registered farmers;
- members of the free professions (lawyers, notaries, translators,
  freelance software developers, etc.);
- sole traders;
- partners in commercial companies who personally work for the company.

## Monthly insurable income

The self-employed person chooses their monthly insurable income within the
statutory minimum and maximum set annually by the State Social Insurance
budget law. The choice can be changed during the year by notifying the
National Revenue Agency.

## Contribution components

The self-employed person pays, at their own expense, contributions for:
- State Social Insurance — mandatory at least for the Pensions fund;
  optional for the General Illness and Maternity fund;
- Supplementary Mandatory Pension Insurance for individuals born after
  31 December 1959;
- Health Insurance.

The exact percentages are set by the State Social Insurance budget law and
the Health Insurance Act and may change year to year.

## Payment deadline

Contributions for a given month are due by the 25th of the following month.
For example, January contributions are due by 25 February.

## Annual reconciliation

In the annual personal income tax return (Article 50 of the PIT Act), the
self-employed person declares actual taxable income and pays final social
security contributions if the actual income exceeds the chosen insurable
income.
