# Support and service levels

## How to reach support

- **Free** — the community forum and email.
- **Plus** — email and in-app chat.
- **Team** — email, in-app chat and a named contact for the workspace
  administrators.

The support desk is staffed on working days; its opening hours and the target
time to a first answer for each plan are reference facts.

## Uptime commitment

Plus and Team come with a monthly uptime commitment. Planned maintenance is
announced at least 48 hours in advance on the status page and is not counted as
downtime.

## Service credits

When the Team uptime commitment is missed in a calendar month, the workspace
receives a service credit on the next invoice, as a percentage of that month's
fee. Credits are applied automatically; no claim is needed.

## What support does not do

Support does not write notes on a customer's behalf, read the content of notes,
or change billing details without the billing contact's confirmation.
