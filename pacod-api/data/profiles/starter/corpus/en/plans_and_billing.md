# Plans, pricing and billing

Nimbus Notes is a fictional online note-taking service. It exists to show how an
agent on this platform answers from its knowledge base and its reference facts.

## The three plans

- **Free** — for personal use. One user, a limited number of notes, community
  and email support.
- **Plus** — for individuals who need more storage, larger attachments and a
  longer version history. Unlimited notes.
- **Team** — for organisations. Shared workspaces, admin controls, single
  sign-on, the strongest uptime commitment and the fastest support.

Prices are set per user and per month. The current amounts, the discount for
annual billing and the length of the free trial are kept as reference facts, so
the help desk always quotes the figure in force.

## Trials

Plus and Team can be tried free of charge. No card is needed to start a trial;
at the end of the trial the workspace falls back to Free unless a plan is
chosen. Nothing is deleted when a trial ends — notes above the Free limits become
read-only until the account is upgraded or the notes are removed.

## How billing works

- Monthly subscriptions are invoiced once a month, on a fixed day of the month,
  for the number of users active on that day.
- Annual subscriptions are invoiced once, up front, at the discounted annual
  price.
- Adding a user in the middle of a period is charged pro rata on the next
  invoice; removing a user takes effect at the next renewal.
- Invoices are available as PDF under **Settings → Billing → Invoices** and are
  also sent to the billing contact by email.

## Refunds

A first paid charge can be refunded in full within the refund window, no
questions asked. After that, payments are not refunded, but a subscription can
be cancelled at any time and stays active until the end of the paid period.

## Price changes

Prices are reviewed once a year. Customers are told before a price change takes
effect; an annual subscription keeps its price until its renewal date.
